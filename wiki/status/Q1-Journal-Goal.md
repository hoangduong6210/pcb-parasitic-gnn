# Q1 Journal Goal and Progress Gates

## Owner instruction and scope

On 2026-10-03 the owner explicitly requested a continuing goal toward a Q1
journal and authorized proceeding with the project. IEEE TCAD remains the
preferred target. The controllable deliverable is a scientifically defensible,
reproducible submission-ready manuscript package; editorial acceptance cannot
be guaranteed or declared by this project. Journal quartile must be checked
against a named ranking system, category and year before submission. The owner
was asked whether JCR or SJR is the relevant criterion and replied to proceed
with TCAD research. Venue focus is therefore settled for ongoing work; the
ranking-system choice is deferred and does not block research. TCAD's current
quartile is not asserted here.

This standing instruction permits sequential research implementation,
no-solver verification, bounded SLURM studies, preservation, wiki maintenance
and repository publication. New studies still require versioned protocols,
predeclared estimands, data splits, resource ceilings and immutable source
before execution. A failed experiment is evidence, not permission to change
its gates, discard a geometry or retry with a larger cap. Broad corpus or
training expansion waits for its scientific prerequisites and a new frozen
protocol; no earlier `automatic_expansion: false` receipt is rewritten.

Keep meshing, solving, training and substantive numerical replay off login
nodes. Preserve older studies and released paper snapshots. Local internal
agent guidance remains untracked. Paper content is derived from admitted wiki
snapshots, with the actual Codex assistance disclosed. Human scientific
admission remains distinct from software validation. Formal submission,
author declarations, coauthor approval, copyright transfer and financial
commitments require the owner's confirmation.
