# Manuscript AI Disclosure

The author identified OpenAI Codex as the tool used for manuscript outlining,
wording organization, and sentence suggestions. The following statement is
included in the Acknowledgment of the
[TCAD working manuscript](../../Paper/Paper_TCAD/main.tex), before References:

> OpenAI Codex was used to assist with outlining the manuscript, organizing and refining its wording, and suggesting sentence formulations. The authors are responsible for the final content of the manuscript.

The statement records the reported writing assistance. It does not assert that
all historical code, figures or research activities were free of AI assistance.
The authors should update it if the actual scope changes during preparation.

The [TCAD instructions](https://ieee-ceda.org/publications/tcad/tcad-paper-submissions)
require authors to identify whether and how generative AI was used in an
Acknowledgments statement. The released Journal Snapshot 1.1.1 is retained
unchanged; this disclosure belongs to the new working draft.
